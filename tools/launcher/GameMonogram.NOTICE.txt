TSFP monogram letter shapes

The outlined TSFP lettering in GameMonogram.cs was typeset with Future
TimeSplitters by Chequered Ink, then exported as native vector geometry.

Author's page: https://www.dafont.com/future-timesplitters.font
Published author note: "Inspired by the excellent TimeSplitters series of games.
Free for all use!"

This is a fan-made, series-inspired typeface, not the official logo font.
Only the typeset four-letter monogram is included as geometry. The original
font file is kept with local provenance and is not bundled or needed to build.
The complete game title and enlarged Future Perfect subtitle still use the
original logo image supplied locally, rather than this typeface.
