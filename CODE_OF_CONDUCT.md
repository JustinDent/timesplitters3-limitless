# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome, whether you are playing, asking questions, reporting a
problem or contributing code. These rules apply to this project's issues,
pull requests and Discussions.

## Be respectful

- Be patient with newcomers and people with different experience or backgrounds.
- Keep feedback constructive. Critique ideas and code, not the person.
- No harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks or sexual harassment.
- No spam, doxxing or sharing someone else's private information.
- Respect moderators' decisions and other people's boundaries.

## Report a problem

Use the content's **... → Report content** menu. Choose **Report to repository
admins** if available, or report abuse to GitHub Support. GitHub explains the
options in its [reporting guide](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
For a public concern that is safe to raise openly, you can also mention
**@TheDoffman** in the relevant thread. Do not repeat abusive content or post
private details publicly. Reports sent to GitHub Support are handled by GitHub.

## Moderation

Maintainers may remove content, give a warning, lock a conversation or restrict
participation depending on the seriousness and repetition of the behavior.
Reports will be considered in context; immediate restrictions may be needed
for threats, harassment or exposure of private information.

Software vulnerabilities belong in the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
